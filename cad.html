01 / IMPORT & DOCUMENTSTL · OBJ · 3MF

Supported: binary/ASCII STL, basic OBJ triangles and triangulated 3MF meshes. 8 MB maximum. No changes are made to your file.

STL and OBJ typically do not encode units. The original source is unchanged; choose the units used in the authoring program. 3MF declares units in its XML when available.
