02 / PHYSICS REFERENCEEDUCATIONAL NOTES

Concepts behind a flight log

These relationships help explain recorded motion, without creating a launch plan.

AIR RESISTANCE

Drag

Drag grows with the square of the object's speed relative to air. Its direction opposes that motion.

FD = ½ ρ CD A v²
MOTOR PERFORMANCE

Total impulse

Impulse describes the area under a force-versus-time graph. Burn time identifies the interval over which force is applied.

I = ∫ F(t) dt
MOTION

Acceleration

Acceleration measures how velocity changes over time and is related to net force through Newton's second law.

a = Fnet / m
FLIGHT DATA

Mass and measurements

Actual motion depends on many interacting variables. Saved values should be interpreted alongside the original measurements and model assumptions.
