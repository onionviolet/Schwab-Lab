02 / RESEARCH PRINCIPLES

What changes in a measured flight?

Recorded position, velocity and acceleration describe distinct quantities. A measurement can have uncertainty and a reference frame; the direction of a vector alone does not establish a safe or controllable trajectory.

r(t) → v(t) → a(t)

These are descriptive mathematical relationships, not steering rules.

03 / CONSTRAINTS

Software boundary
